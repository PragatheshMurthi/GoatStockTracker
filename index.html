Overview

Track your goats with confidence

GoatStockTracker is a lightweight tool designed for goat farm operators who want a practical way to manage livestock details and feed-related records. The project is now presented as a GitHub Pages site so it is easy to share with farm staff and collaborators.

Stock tracking

Monitor goat inventory and maintain a clear view of each animal or group.

Feed management

Log feeding activity and keep the day-to-day nutrition plan organized.

Simple workflow

Use a clean, easy-to-understand interface that supports everyday farm tasks.
